2026–27 BAT Doubles

Team pre-registration Registration details

Season structure

Three consecutive stages

Who can play

Divisions & eligibility

Each doubles team may include 2–4 players. Players may register for up to two divisions; a third registration requires coordinator approval. The UTR difference between doubles partners cannot exceed 3.5, or 2.5 in Platinum and Diamond.

For divisions with stated limits, the combined UTR of the two players is the Team Power Index (TPI) and must stay within the inclusive range.

All groups except MuLan are open and are not divided by gender. Yahua Cup divisions are for junior players only. Teams may be men’s doubles, women’s doubles, or mixed doubles, and players may indicate a preferred format, geographic area, or match time when registering.

How it works

Flexible match play

    Season details

    From regular season to championship

    The season runs from November 15, 2026 through March 31, 2027. The announcement describes the regular season as November 2026 through January 2027, followed by playoffs and finals in February and March. It also lists “February 2026 – March 2027” for the latter stages; the February year should be confirmed by the organizing team.

    After the group stage, playoff draws are arranged using group rankings and player levels. Winning playoff teams qualify for Championship Weekend at a location with rented courts.

    Only players who have played at least one regular-season match are eligible for playoffs and championship rounds. Default or unplayed matches do not count. Teams whose combined UTR significantly exceeds their division’s upper limit may be moved up for playoffs.

    Teams & scheduling

    Build your team

    Each team may include two to four players. Teams can choose a custom name; if no name is provided, the default combines the players’ last names.

    Teams may register as a Dynamic Duo, Energetic Trio, or Fantastic Four. Each player acts as their own captain by scheduling matches and entering or editing scores directly in the UTR Team League platform.

    All matches must be mutually scheduled. When there is a conflict, the home team must propose three possible time slots on different days, including at least one weekday evening and one weekend option, during reasonable hours. The visiting team chooses one. Court or facility fees must be agreed upon in advance.

    Official extensions may be granted for extended rain, New Year, or Lunar New Year holidays. Vacations, work trips, and injuries do not automatically justify postponements; teams should reschedule early or use substitutes.

    Formats & conduct

    Match formats

    Adult groups play one doubles line in each team match. Yahua Cup allows up to one doubles line and two singles lines per team match. Adult matches are best of three sets, with the third set allowed to be a 10-point tiebreak. Yahua Cup junior matches may use Fast 4, Pro Set, or best of three sets, with or without ad scoring.

    Cross-group challenge matches are encouraged for teams that have the capacity. For junior matches, parents and guardians may not coach or officiate during play. If both sides are juniors, brief coaching may be allowed before the third set.

    Players using self-rated or projected UTRs should estimate realistically using the conversion chart. UTR is determined at registration. If a player’s rating changes significantly, the organizing committee may adjust eligibility or move the team to a higher division.

    Changes & awards

    Throughout the season

    Teams may replace players mid-season for injury or extended travel, provided replacements have comparable skill and the roster remains at four players or fewer. Players with inaccurate self-ratings, or rated players whose level significantly changes, may switch divisions with coordinator approval.

    Players who withdraw before playing any matches receive a full refund.

    • Champion: custom gold medals, cash prize, and gifts.
    • Runner-up: custom silver medals, cash prize, and gifts.
    • Third place: custom bronze medals, cash prize, and gifts.
    • Consolation bracket finalists: custom medals and gifts.

    Before you register

    A flexible league needs flexibility

    Please assess your availability and other commitments, including USTA and UTR leagues, before registering. Incomplete matches and scheduling difficulties affect the experience for everyone.

    This is a flex league built on communication and cooperation. Players should be proactive and willing to coordinate and compromise; players who cannot do so are advised not to register.

    Registration

    Save your spot

    Start with team pre-registration. Submit with at least two players and indicate the desired division, team name, player names, and other information. Official registration will be completed on the UTR Team League platform after teams are created.
