About us

More than a match

Bay Area Tennis was founded in 2010, bringing players together through singles and doubles leagues, team tournaments, family events, and regional competitions.

Rooted in the Bay Area, our community connects with tennis communities across Northern California, Southern California, and the Pacific Coast.

  • Recreational & competitive players
  • Families & junior players
  • U.S. & Chinese university alumni
  • Tech professionals & industry peers
  • Regional tennis exchange teams
