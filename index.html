A community in motion

More than a match.

Bay Area Tennis has brought players together since 2019 through singles and doubles leagues, team tournaments, family events, and regional competitions.

We connect players throughout the Bay Area and with tennis communities across Northern California, Southern California, and the Pacific Coast. Whether you come to compete, meet your next doubles partner, or bring the family, there is a place for you on court.

Play your way.

Flexible formats and welcoming competition for every kind of tennis community.

01 / SINGLES

BAT Singles Tournament

Flexible matches across the Bay Area, with adult, women’s, junior, and different UTR-level divisions.

02 / DOUBLES

BAT Doubles League

A season-long doubles competition with flexible scheduling.

03 / TEAM LEAGUE

Summer UTR Team League

Weekly team competition on the UTR Team League platform.

04 / ALUMNI

Zijing Cup Alumni Team Championship

Held since 2016, with Gold and Silver divisions in men’s, women’s, and mixed doubles.

05 / FAMILY

Fotile Cup Family Championship

Couples, parent-child teams, siblings, and multigenerational families meet on court.

06 / COMMUNITY

Silicon Valley Technology Elite Tournament

A gathering for technology professionals, founders, executives, professors, and alumni teams.

07 / REGIONAL

More ways to compete

Regional community tournaments, Pacific Coast invitationals, Northern–Southern California exchanges, mixed doubles, and Junior Team Tennis.
