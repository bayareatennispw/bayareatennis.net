# PureWave · Bay Area Tennis

Swing the Pure Wave Within

Year-round tennis leagues, tournaments, and community events connecting players, families, university alumni, and professionals across the Bay Area.

## About Us

Bay Area Tennis has operated since 2010, bringing players together through singles and doubles leagues, team tournaments, family events, and regional competitions.

Rooted in the Bay Area, our community connects with tennis communities across Northern California, Southern California, and the Pacific Coast.

## Our Events

- **BAT Singles Tournament:** Flexible matches across the Bay Area, with divisions for adults, women, juniors, and different UTR levels.
- **BAT Doubles League:** A season-long doubles competition with flexible scheduling.
- **Summer UTR Team League:** Weekly team competition on the UTR Team League platform.
- **Zijing Cup Alumni Team Championship:** Held since 2016, featuring Gold and Silver divisions with men’s, women’s, and mixed doubles.
- **Fotile Cup Family Championship:** Tennis for couples, parent-child teams, siblings, and multigenerational families.
- **Silicon Valley Technology Elite Tournament:** Bringing together technology professionals, founders, executives, professors, and alumni teams.
- **More Competitions:** Regional community tournaments, Pacific Coast invitationals, Northern–Southern California exchanges, mixed doubles events, and Junior Team Tennis.

Each event includes dates, location, divisions, eligibility, entry fees, registration deadlines, registration links, and results.
